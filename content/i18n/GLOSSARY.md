# 번역 용어집 · Translation glossary

터닝포인트는 **한국 고등학교 3년**을 주사위로 보내는 게임이다. 배경은 번역해도 한국 고등학교 그대로 둔다
(수능·학원·급식 같은 한국 학교 문화가 게임의 재미다). 다른 나라 학교 제도로 바꾸지 않는다.

Turning Point is a dice game about three years at a **Korean high school**. Keep the Korean setting in every
language (CSAT, cram schools, school lunch…); do not localize it into another country's school system.

## 문체 · Tone

- **ko (원문)**: 따뜻하고 장난스러운 파스텔 일상물. 사건 글은 짧은 평서문("~했다").
- **en**: casual, warm, playful. Short sentences. Narration in past tense like the source ("You missed the bus." / "The bus doors closed right in front of you."). Second person is fine. American spelling.
- **ja**: やわらかく明るい日常系。地の文は常体（〜した／〜だ）。選択肢は短い動詞句（「全力で走る」）。
- **zh (简体)**: 轻松温暖、带点俏皮的校园日常。叙述用简洁口语。选项用短动词短语（"全力冲刺"）。

## 길이 · Length

Cards are small. Keep option labels (`.label`) short — roughly the same visual width as the Korean:
en ≤ ~28 characters, ja ≤ ~16, zh ≤ ~12. Titles (`.title`) and names (`.name`) short too.
Event text (`.text`, `resultText.*`) can be one or two sentences.

## 꼭 지킬 것 · Rules

- Keep every emoji, number, `+`/`−` sign, `%`, quotes and line break (`\n`) exactly where they make sense.
- Keep `{name}` style placeholders unchanged (messages only). Never translate keys.
- Money is Korean won: ko `30,000원`, en `₩30,000`, ja `30,000ウォン`, zh `30,000韩元`.
